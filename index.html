How it works

ChainGrade

Your academic work, permanently recorded. Verified by anyone, anywhere.

Submit your project once and get a permanent record on the blockchain. Any employer, institution, or reviewer can check it instantly — without calling your university, without waiting, without middlemen.

The problem

Credentials expire. Records shouldn't.

Academic records are only as reliable as the institution that holds them. If a university closes, loses accreditation, or simply doesn't respond to a verification request, your credential becomes unverifiable overnight. That's a structural flaw — not an edge case.

ChainGrade puts each submission on a public blockchain. The record belongs to you, not the issuer. It stays there permanently, and anyone can verify it without needing permission from anyone.

Three steps

01

Connect your wallet

Your wallet address becomes your permanent identity on the network. No email, no password. Just a cryptographic key you already control.

→
02

Upload your project

Your file goes to IPFS — a distributed storage network — and a unique fingerprint of it is recorded on-chain. Alter even one character and the fingerprint no longer matches.

→
03

Faculty verify it

Faculty reviewers each sign off with their own wallet. Every signature is recorded on-chain. Anyone can audit who approved, when they approved, and exactly what they signed.

→
⬖

Ready? Connect your wallet to begin.

ChainGrade runs on Polygon Amoy (testnet). You'll need MetaMask or any compatible wallet extension. Your public address becomes your identity — no sign-up required.

ChainGrade never stores your private key. All signatures are produced locally by your wallet. Only your public address and signed assertions are submitted to the ledger.
